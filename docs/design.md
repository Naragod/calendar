# Why the poster looks this way

Each mark on the poster uses one cue, and no two marks share a cue. So a holiday on a Saturday can be red and bold at once, and both still read.

| What it marks | Its cue |
|---|---|
| The time of year | The colour of the box |
| Weekends | Bold numbers |
| Public holidays | A red number and a red name |
| The last day of a month | A dark corner |
| Your own dates | A navy name |

## Weekends: bold numbers

Bold stands out at a glance, even for people who are colour-blind ([Healey](https://www.csc2.ncsu.edu/faculty/healey/PP/index.html)). It also prints well in black and white.

A darker shade on weekend boxes was hard to spot. The box colours already change each week, so the shade got lost ([colour-science](https://colour.readthedocs.io/en/v0.4.1/generated/colour.difference.JND_CIE1976.html)).

Coloured numbers clash with some weeks of the rainbow. Some colour-blind readers see red numbers as black ([Tottori Prefecture](https://www.pref.tottori.lg.jp/secure/954780/22_04.pdf)).

## Holidays: red letters

Calendars have printed feast days in red since at least 1490. That is where "red-letter day" comes from ([Phrases.org.uk](https://phrases.org.uk/meanings/red-letter-day.html)).

The red is `#b32322`. It reads as red, not as a thick black line. It keeps a contrast of 3:1 or more against the black lines and on every box colour. That is the bar for large text ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)).

The name sits at the bottom, centred. It is the same size as the other labels. Each Ontario holiday name fits on one line.

## White tiles: how to read the poster

All the help sits in one place, at the start of the first row ([Bertin](https://www.esri.com/content/dam/esrisites/en-us/esri-press/pdfs/semiology-graphics-diagrams-networks-maps-sample-chapter.pdf)). Each sample looks like a real box, not a plain swatch ([Datawrapper](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/color-keys-for-data-visualizations)). The tiles sit beside 1 January. In 2027, the first three days show a holiday and a weekend.

## Text: three sizes, one gap

The poster has three text sizes. They are for the title, the big day number and the labels ([Butterick](https://practicaltypography.com/headings.html)). Every label sits 9 pt from the box border, on every side.

## Before you print

Print a strip at full size. Look at it from 0.5 m and 2 m away. Ask the printer to print black text with black ink alone, so small text stays sharp ([4over4](https://www.4over4.com/guide/cmyk-values-for-print-explained)).
